# Mechanical PCB study — not the Rev.A electrical board

`mechanical-study.kicad_pcb` is a **real KiCad PCB file** with a provisional 40 mm × 40 mm outline. It has no placed electronic components, no copper traces and no drilled holes. This provides a tangible editable starting point for mechanical layout only.

The 40 mm × 40 mm dimensions are an engineering placeholder, **not a user-approved enclosure dimension**. The release workflows deliberately do not accept this file as `Paky76-RevA.kicad_pcb`.
